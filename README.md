# Personal Improvement Devotional

## Overview
This devotional is inspired by Franklin Covey's Focus workshops. It helps users align their values with goals and create a weekly plan to "sharpen the saw" in four key areas: Social, Spiritual, Physical, and Learning/Mental. It also includes a role-planning system to visualize how one's roles impact others.

## Values
Define your core values. These are the principles that guide your decisions and actions.

- **Example Values**:
  - Integrity
  - Family
  - Health
  - Growth

---

## Goals
Create goals that align with your values. Goals should be specific, measurable, and time-bound.

### Example Goals:
1. **Social**: Connect with a friend or family member weekly.
2. **Spiritual**: Spend 10 minutes daily in meditation or prayer.
3. **Physical**: Exercise for 30 minutes, 4 times a week.
4. **Learning/Mental**: Read one book per month.

---

## Weekly Plan
Plan your week to incorporate activities that align with your goals.

| Day       | Social          | Spiritual       | Physical        | Learning/Mental |
|-----------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Monday    | Call a friend   | Morning prayer  | 30-min workout  | Read 10 pages   |
| Tuesday   | Family dinner   | Evening walk    | Yoga session    | Watch a tutorial|

---

## Role Planning
Visualize how your roles impact others. Define your roles and the expectations associated with them.

### Example Roles:
1. **Parent**: Support and nurture children.
2. **Employee**: Contribute to team success.
3. **Friend**: Be a good listener and offer support.
